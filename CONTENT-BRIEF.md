# Wazely Website Content Brief

## Brand hierarchy

- Company/platform: **Wazely**
- Current flagship product: **Wazely CRM**
- Product destination: `https://crm.wazely.io`
- Customer help destination: `/knowledge-base/`

Wazely must look like a company that can publish more products later. Do not make the entire company identity equal to only one CRM product, but give Wazely CRM the strongest visual priority because it is the product currently being promoted.

## Suggested core message

Eyebrow: `WAZELY CRM — OUR FLAGSHIP PRODUCT`

Hero title: `Turn every customer conversation into growth.`

Hero description: `Wazely CRM brings customer conversations, campaigns, contacts, automation, and team collaboration into one connected workspace.`

Primary CTA: `Explore Wazely CRM`

Secondary CTA: `View Knowledge Base`

## Page structure

1. Sticky company navigation
2. Company-level hero featuring Wazely CRM
3. Flagship product overview with the CRM dashboard screenshot
4. Feature story: shared team inbox
5. Feature story: campaigns and analytics
6. Feature story: visual automation
7. Compact capability grid
8. Product portfolio section designed to accept future products
9. Knowledge-base/resource callout
10. Final CRM CTA
11. Company footer

## Verified capability themes

- Shared customer inbox and conversation management
- Contact directory, tags, segments, and custom data
- WhatsApp templates and broadcast campaigns
- Delivery, read, and campaign performance reporting
- Visual automation and chatbot flows
- Agent assignment, notes, and team collaboration
- WhatsApp Business connection
- Appointment booking and WhatsApp forms
- Product catalogs and ecommerce workflows
- Integrations and developer tools

Use restrained language. Do not claim exact customer counts, revenue results, conversion increases, uptime, Meta partnership status, pricing, or review scores unless the business supplies verified evidence.

## Visual direction

- Font: Mona Sans from `assets/mona-sans.woff2`
- Brand accent: cyan-to-violet gradient from the Wazely icon
- Product accent: clean emerald/teal used by the CRM interface
- Base colors: deep navy text, white and soft neutral surfaces
- Style: premium SaaS, spacious, confident, technically credible
- Use real product imagery in framed browser windows; do not cover important UI with decorative elements.

## Future products

Show Wazely CRM as `Available now`. The product-area layout should support more cards later, but do not invent names or features for unreleased products. A single neutral `More Wazely products are coming` card is acceptable.
